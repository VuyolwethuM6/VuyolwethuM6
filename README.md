## 🔭 What I'm Building Right Now

### 🎓 [Viva Tutorials Platform](https://vivatutorials.co.za)
A fully custom, AI-integrated tutoring management system I designed, built, and operate — serving 24 active students in Grades 8–12. Built entirely on Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, and Vercel.

**Features shipped:**
- Student Portal, Parent Portal, and Admin Dashboard — three distinct role-based interfaces from a single deployment
- Finance system with automated PDF invoice/receipt generation and email delivery
- MarkAI — an AI-powered student marking and feedback engine (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
- Google Classroom integration, NSC exam countdown widget, automated monthly/weekly billing triggers
- Parent authentication system, mark tracking, and attendance management

> *This platform is a live proof of concept for Viva Academy — my long-term vision to build a scalable, systemised community education institution across South Africa.*
